Stash Support

Support for Stash on iOS, iPadOS, and macOS

If you need help with Stash, please contact us by email.

Contact

Email: output@unwraps.net

When Contacting Support

Please include the following information when possible:

  • Your device model
  • Your OS version
  • The Stash app version
  • A short description of the issue
  • Steps to reproduce the issue, if available

Privacy

Please do not include passwords, secret snippets, private clipboard contents, or other sensitive information in support emails.

Privacy Policy
